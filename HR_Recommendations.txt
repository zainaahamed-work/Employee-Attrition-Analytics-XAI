HR ATTRITION RECOMMENDATIONS
==============================

1. Key factors linked to attrition (from SHAP)
   1. OverTime (importance 0.050): Review overtime workload and staffing levels. Overtime is the strongest attrition driver in this analysis.
   2. StockOptionLevel (importance 0.028): Extend stock option or long-term incentive schemes to more employees, as low stock option levels are linked with leaving.
   3. MonthlyIncome (importance 0.024): Check pay against market rates, especially for lower-paid and early-career roles.
   4. Age (importance 0.020): Offer career-growth and mentoring programmes aimed at younger employees, who leave more often.
   5. TotalWorkingYears (importance 0.017): Create clearer progression paths for employees with limited total experience.

2. Combinations to watch (from Apriori rules)
   - Low income + New (2 yrs or less): 36.6% of these employees left (lift 2.27).
   - Works overtime: 30.5% of these employees left (lift 1.89).
   - New (2 yrs or less): 29.8% of these employees left (lift 1.85).

3. Note
   These findings show patterns in past data, not guaranteed causes. Use them to guide conversations and further review.
